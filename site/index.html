Abstract

Large language models can reconsider uncertain classifications, but re-evaluating every input increases generation workload and can introduce new errors. We study confidence-guided selective re-evaluation for three-class emotion classification in social media. A temperature-scaled DistilBERT classifier retains high-confidence predictions and routes the remaining posts to Llama 2 or Llama 3 using Direct, chain-of-thought (CoT), or task-adapted SELF-DISCOVER prompts.

On the exploratory 12,000-post Reddit evaluation, Llama 3 SELF-DISCOVER improves accuracy from 96.92% to 97.20% while re-evaluating 1.82% of posts. On a separate, fixed-protocol evaluation of 9,000 posts, it produces 17 net corrections while routing 1.23%, with a significant improvement over Phase 1 under the specified Holm adjustment. SELF-DISCOVER has the highest observed accuracy across the evaluated sets, but its differences from Direct and CoT are not statistically resolved on Reddit. The results describe a proxy-label classification task, not clinical diagnosis.

Method

Confidence-based routing

Phase 1 predicts Depression, Neutral, or Happy. Temperature scaling adjusts the model's confidence, and a threshold determines which predictions are retained. The selected threshold is τ = 0.70: 11,807 of 12,000 calibration posts are accepted, with 1.940% empirical error and a 2.162% one-sided Clopper–Pearson upper bound.

The lowest candidate in the prespecified 0.70–1.00 grid already satisfies the 5% risk budget, so the grid boundary determines the operating point. Temperature fitting and threshold selection use the same calibration partition. The pointwise bound is not a simultaneous post-selection guarantee and does not guarantee the same risk under distribution shift.

Selective LLM re-evaluation

Direct uses one generation call. CoT uses five dialogue turns, including acknowledgment and text-response turns. SELF-DISCOVER constructs a task-level plan from the class policy and 18 researcher-authored reasoning modules, then reuses that plan for a fresh evaluation of each routed post. Plan construction does not receive evaluation posts or their reference labels.

The protocols differ in label visibility, call structure, and generation budget; the comparison is between complete configurations rather than an isolated test of reasoning components. If the final label cannot be parsed, the Phase 1 prediction is retained.

Prompts and saved plans

Prompts are instructions; saved plans are model outputs. Direct asks for one label. CoT divides re-evaluation into five calls. SELF-DISCOVER first builds a reusable task-level plan, then applies it to each routed post.

Source file

Direct receives the post, shared classification policy, and Phase 1 label. It returns a final label without a rationale.

{policy} is the shared label policy; {text} is one post; {phase1_label} is the classifier's prediction.

You are an expert annotator for research-oriented, non-clinical emotion classification.

{policy}

Text:
{text}

The Phase 1 classifier predicted: {phase1_label}

Select the final label that best represents the text. Return no rationale and end with exactly one line in this form:
Final label: Depression
or Final label: Neutral
or Final label: Happy

Original files: prompts · saved plans.

Results

The main Reddit and synthetic Mixed Emotion sets were used during prompt development and are exploratory evaluations. The additional 9,000-post run keeps model weights, temperature, threshold, prompts, and the saved plan fixed.

What changes after re-evaluation?

In the fixed 9,000-post evaluation, only 111 posts reached the LLM. SELF-DISCOVER corrected 30 errors but changed 13 correct predictions to errors: 17 more correct predictions overall, or +0.19 percentage points. The other 8,889 predictions stayed with Phase 1.

On the additional evaluation, the exact McNemar p-value for SELF-DISCOVER versus Phase 1 is 0.0137 (Holm-adjusted p = 0.0412 within the three-test family). Direct and CoT each yield 13 net corrections. Their accuracy differences from SELF-DISCOVER are not significant (both Holm-adjusted p = 0.9614).

Recorded model-generation time on an NVIDIA L4 is 54.9 s for Direct, 3,116.4 s for CoT, and 2,568.5 s for SELF-DISCOVER. Thus, Direct produces four fewer net corrections than SELF-DISCOVER at about 2.1% of its recorded generation time. These measurements cover generation, not total pipeline runtime.

Data and annotation

The Reddit labels are community-derived proxies for Depression, Neutral, and Happy. The 300-example synthetic Mixed Emotion set tests mixed or shifting affect under a shared classification policy. It was generated using GPT-5 via Codex and researcher-authored scenario and label constraints, and also informed prompt development.

Two researchers uninvolved in data generation or Phase 2 prompt development independently annotated all 300 examples without seeing targets, scenario metadata, model outputs, or each other's responses. They agreed on 277 examples (92.33%; unweighted Cohen's κ = 0.8847). Agreement with the original targets was 96.00% and 96.33%. A third researcher checked completeness, record alignment, and statistics. No adjudication or post-audit relabeling was performed.

Scope

Subreddit-derived labels can carry topic and lexical biases. The annotation audit supports consistency with the shared policy, not clinical validity or generalization to real-world populations. The results do not establish a universal advantage of SELF-DISCOVER, the isolated contribution of its planning and evidence checks, or generalization to newer models and other platforms.

Reproducibility

The public repository includes frozen configurations, exact prompts, saved plans, synthetic data, text-free predictions, aggregate reports, and verification scripts. Raw Reddit text, user identifiers, model weights, and the manuscript PDF are not redistributed here.
