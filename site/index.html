01 · Overview

Selective re-evaluation, measured one correction at a time.

This study evaluates a two-stage classifier for subreddit-derived proxy emotion labels. Temperature-scaled DistilBERT predictions with confidence at least 0.70 are retained; the remaining posts are re-evaluated by Llama 2 or Llama 3 using Direct, chain-of-thought (CoT), or task-adapted SELF-DISCOVER prompts.

Evaluation separates error concentration by the router from net corrections by the LLM. The main Reddit and synthetic Mixed Emotion sets informed prompt development. A separate 9,000-post evaluation used the frozen configuration and found a gain over Phase 1 under the specified Holm adjustment. SELF-DISCOVER had the highest observed accuracy, while its differences from Direct and CoT remained unresolved on Reddit.

Routing

Finite-sample risk check

Temperature scaling and a one-sided Clopper–Pearson upper bound define the accepted set.

Reasoning

Accounting

A separate 9,000-post run keeps the classifier, threshold, prompts, and cached plan fixed.

03 · Calibration & routing

The threshold controls who keeps the Phase 1 label.

Temperature scaling reduces overconfidence. Threshold selection then maximizes calibration-set coverage among candidates whose one-sided accepted-risk upper bound is at most 5%.

Selected threshold τ = 0.70

The lowest value in the prespecified 0.70–1.00 grid.

Why “non-binding”?

The lowest tested threshold, 0.70, already satisfied the risk criterion, so the grid boundary determined the selected operating point. The same calibration partition fitted temperature and selected the threshold; pointwise bounds do not provide a simultaneous post-selection or out-of-distribution risk guarantee.

04 · Phase 2 protocols

Three ways to reconsider the same routed post.

Each protocol uses the same class policy and routed input. They differ in dialogue structure, Phase 1-label visibility, and generation workload.

05 · Evidence

Frozen evaluation and independent label audit.

The additional Reddit evaluation tests the final configuration without further tuning. A separate audit tests whether the synthetic Mixed Emotion targets follow the shared three-class policy.

Fixed additional evaluation

9,000 Reddit posts

+17 net corrections

Llama 3 SELF-DISCOVER improved accuracy from 97.58% to 97.77% while routing 111 posts (1.23%). Its exact McNemar p-value versus Phase 1 was 0.0137; the Holm-adjusted value was 0.0412.

Direct and CoT each produced +13 net corrections. Their accuracy differences from SELF-DISCOVER were not statistically significant.

07 · Scope

What the evidence does and does not support.

Supported here

  • Confidence routing can concentrate Phase 1 errors.
  • Selective LLM re-evaluation can produce positive net corrections.
  • Corrections, introduced errors, workload, and paired tests can be audited.
  • The released Mixed Emotion targets show high agreement under the shared policy.

Not established

  • Clinical validity, diagnosis, screening, or treatment utility.
  • Generalization to other platforms, label schemes, or newer/larger LLMs.
  • A universal advantage of SELF-DISCOVER over Direct or CoT.
  • The isolated causal contribution of planning or evidence-checking components.
