What each equation measures

Temperature-scaled softmax

qk(T)=ezk/T∑j=1Kezj/T

The logits z are the model's scores. Dividing every score by the same positive temperature changes their separation, not their ordering. Higher T softens the distribution; lower T sharpens it.

Negative log-likelihood

NLL=−1N∑i=1Nlnqi,yi

Only the probability assigned to the reference class enters each example's loss. A probability of 0.9 gives a loss of 0.105; 0.01 gives 4.605. Confident wrong predictions receive a large penalty. NLL is the objective used to fit temperature in the study.

Multiclass Brier score

Brier=1N∑i=1N∑k=1K(qi,k−yi,k)2

The reference is one-hot: 1 for the correct class and 0 for the others. This version sums squared errors over classes and averages over examples; it is not divided by the number of classes. Its range is 0 to 2.

Expected calibration error

ECE=∑b=1BnbN|accuracyb−confidenceb|

Group predictions by their maximum probability. In each bin, compare the average confidence with the fraction correct, then weight that gap by the bin's sample share. ECE depends on binning and can hide within-bin differences. Minimizing NLL does not necessarily minimize ECE or Brier.

The manuscript uses 15 equal-width bins for its reliability diagnostics. Temperature was fitted on the calibration partition, not by changing the test labels. The learned temperature and routing threshold were then fixed for evaluation. This tutorial uses five bins and invented scores solely to expose the mechanics.
